Privacy notice

Booking a discovery call with HSJ Tuition. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

HSJ Tuition provides A-Level Chemistry tuition. If you have any question about your data, email info@hsjtuition.co.uk.

What we collect on this page

  • Your name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your teen's first name, year group, current grade and target grade.
  • Your answers about when you want support to start, whether you are in a position to invest, and whether you can both attend the call.
  • The call time you choose, and which advert or page brought you here.

Your details are saved when you press Continue on the first step, so that we can follow up if you do not finish booking.

Why we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you about your call by email, text, WhatsApp or phone.
  • To prepare for the call so it is specific to your teen.
  • To contact you about the booking if you started but did not finish.
  • To understand which adverts lead to booked calls.

We rely on our legitimate interest in responding to your enquiry, and on your consent for the advertising cookie.

Who handles it for us

  • Our customer and calendar system (GrowthPilot, built on GoHighLevel).
  • Our website hosting (Vercel) and database (Google Firebase).
  • Google Meet, for the call itself.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram). When a call is booked we tell Meta that a booking happened, using a scrambled (hashed) version of your email and phone number so it can be matched to an advert. The Meta cookie on the page is only set if you accept it.

We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

If you do not become a client, we delete your enquiry details within 12 months. Ask us at any time and we will delete them sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@hsjtuition.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
